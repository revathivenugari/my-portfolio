About

I'm Revathi Venugari — a frontend developer first. Over four-plus years, I've built responsive, accessible interfaces in React, Angular, and Next.js across a range of products, and that's still where my strength and where I do my best work. I enjoy transforming complex ideas into intuitive, high-performing web experiences — developing new features, improving performance, and implementing SEO best practices, taking ownership from concept to deployment.

Great frontend development is more than writing code — it's about creating seamless user experiences while ensuring performance, accessibility, and long-term maintainability.

More recently, I've started taking on complete website builds too — CMS, APIs, and the infrastructure behind the interface — as a natural extension of that frontend strength, alongside managing a large-scale website migration. Underneath it all, I still think like a frontend developer: I thrive in collaborative environments, communicate closely with cross-functional teams, and focus on solutions that are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with business goals.

I'm always excited to take on challenging frontend projects where I can contribute, grow, and help build products people love. If you're looking for a frontend developer who combines strong technical execution with a product-focused mindset, I'd love to connect.
